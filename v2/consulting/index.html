Research Consulting

Advisory and research services drawing on over a decade of expertise in media, technology policy and governance.

What I Offer

I work with international organisations, academic institutions, and civil society bodies on research and advisory projects at the intersection of media, technology, and policy. Drawing on over a decade of academic research and fieldwork across India, Southeast Asia, and the UK, I bring rigorous analytical expertise to questions of AI governance, data policy, gender and technology, and media regulation. Past engagements have included multi-country research projects with the United Nations University and UN Women, as well as country-level expert advisory roles on EU-funded research programmes.
