Teaching

Teaching

I have taught across undergraduate and postgraduate programmes in media studies, communication, and digital culture. My teaching is informed by my research interests in technology, gender, and society, and draws on a range of interdisciplinary perspectives.

Teaching Philosophy

I approach teaching as a collaborative intellectual endeavour — one that encourages students to engage critically with contemporary questions about media, power, and technology. I aim to create inclusive, discussion-led learning environments that value diverse perspectives and forms of knowledge.

Courses

Media, Technology and Society

Postgraduate · Placeholder University · 2023–2024

An introduction to the social shaping of technology, platform economies, and the governance of digital media.

Gender and Digital Culture

Undergraduate · Placeholder University · 2022–2023

Explores intersections of gender, identity, and digital life, including online harassment, algorithmic bias, and feminist approaches to technology.
